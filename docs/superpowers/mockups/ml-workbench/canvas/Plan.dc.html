How the estimate works
Memory: about 16 bytes per parameter for full finetuning, split across GPUs by FSDP. LoRA trains small adapters on frozen weights, so it needs far less.
Time: about 6 × parameters × tokens of compute, at about 40% of GPU peak speed. Cost: GPUs × hours × the rate set on the connector ($2.20 per GPU-hour here).
